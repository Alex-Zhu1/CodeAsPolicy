# Operating knowledge for RoboDojo: Coarse-Code + Fine-Delta manipulation

## 1. Robot frames, reach envelope, and gripper geometry
- **World frame & table**: two 6-DoF ARX-X5 arms (`"left"` at negative `x`, `"right"` at positive `x`), `y` away from the robot, `z` up (metres, degrees). Bases sit at `x = ±0.30, y = -0.45`; table surface is `z = 0.765` m. `robo.task` is authoritative; read object heights from the scene, never from stale observations.
- **TCP vs physical gripper envelope**: motion targets `tcp_pos` (finger-pad centre, `0.145 m` ahead of wrist flange `eef_pos` along `+x_tcp`). Fingertips extend `+1.3 cm` past `tcp_pos` (keep `tcp_z >= 0.780` m when pointing down unless using tabletop contact). Closed pads are `~2.4 cm` wide (`±1.2 cm` along `close_dir`, `~2.0 cm` thick), but `~5–7 cm` behind `tcp_pos` the **fixed jaw-rail crossbar** spans `±5 cm` along `close_dir` even at `opening=0.0`. The protruding **wrist-camera housing** sits on `+z_link6` (`pose.axes()[2]`).
- **Gripper conventions**: commands are signed (`CLOSE=1`, `OPEN=-1`, `KEEP=0`); measured `opening` is `1.0` open / `0.0` closed (`width ≈ 0.002 + 0.109 * opening` m). Minimum closed gap is `GRIP_ZERO_M ≈ 2.3 mm`, so thin parts (`< 3.5 mm`) read `opening <= 0.01` just like empty air—never judge thin-part possession from `opening` alone; verify a short lift in wrist/head views.
- **Orientations & reach**: `pitch` 0 level / 90 straight down; `yaw` 0 away / +90 left (`-x`); `roll` turns about `+x_tcp` (at `pitch=90`, `yaw` sets the jaw-closing line and `roll=0`). Build poses from physical vectors with `robo.pose_from_axes(xyz, point_dir, close_dir, roll_deg=...)` and generate arcs with `pose.rotated(axis, deg, pivot=...)`. Straight-down (`pitch=90`) reach stays low (`y <= 0.0` at `z=0.80`, `y <= -0.05` at `z=0.90`, unreachable near `z=1.0`); `pitch=45..60` reaches farther and higher, level (`pitch=0`) farther still.

## 2. Coarse layer: `execute_motion`, `ik` preflight, and transfer strategy
- **Coarse-Code + Fine-Delta split**: use Coarse Code (`execute_motion` + `ik`) for task logic, arm role selection, and smooth C2 transport to **`2.5–4 cm` pre-action hover poses**; hand all close-range (`<= 4 cm`) physical contact (pickup, handoff clamp, tight alignment, seating, pressing) to Fine Delta (`delta_grasp`, `align_feature`, `nudge`).
- **`execute_motion(intent_id, ...)`**: declare `left`/`right` targets (world TCP `xyz`, `Pose`, or `{"xyz": ..., "angles": [p, y, r]}`), `interaction` (`"free"` / `"approach"` / `"contact"`), optional `waypoints` (continuous C2 curves without intermediate stops), `directions`, `holding` (`"empty"` / `"retain"` / `"keep"` or `{arm: ...}` — never opens/closes), `completion`, and `constraints`. Keep default loose tolerances on `"free"` transitions; reserve tight tolerances for final contact. Use `preview=True` (with `following=[...]`) for multi-leg feasibility checks. Do not wrap `execute_motion` in `once()` (keep `once()` for `set_gripper` and `home`); resume paused intents with `resume_motion(id)`. Do not abandon progress from a cost forecast; pass `reserve_steps` on late-stage calls to protect cleanup steps (~30–40% reserve).
- **Full pose-chain `robo.ik()` preflight (0 steps)**: before grasping any constrained object, preflight the full chain `(grasp, pre_contact, seated, rotated_end)` via `robo.ik(arm, target, seed=..., jaw_symmetry=True, twist_rad=..., check_other_arm=True, continuous=...)`. `ik()` automatically checks table and inter-arm collision envelopes (housing, crossbar, fingers), prefers turning `+z_link6` camera housing away from the other arm via `180°` empty-jaw symmetry, and verifies `joint6` (`±3.1 rad`) twist headroom.
- **Align pointing axis with rotation axis**: point along the required rotation axis (e.g. `pitch=90` pointing straight down for a vertical-axis twist) so rotation runs purely on `joint6` in place instead of sweeping a 6-DOF arc into wrist singularities.
- **Clearance & in-place actions**: when a held object protrudes `L_below` below `tcp_pos`, lift straight up (`+z`) to `tcp_z >= z_obs + L_below + 0.03` m so `object_bottom_z = tcp_z - L_below` clears obstacles before lateral travel. After low contact strokes, retreat straight up (`+z`) or reverse along the contact axis before sweeping sideways. Act in place (tip, flip, slide, press) rather than relocating objects whose position need not change; inside tightly packed rows, use `pitch=90` (keeping the `±5 cm` crossbar above neighbour tops) with `close_dir` aligned to the stroke and bounded single-item contact.
- **Cross-arm transfer & sequential single-arm clearance**: never drive both arms into the shared central zone simultaneously. For compact uniform objects (`< ~6–7 cm` or `< ~4 cm` exposed beyond donor pads), prefer supported **`place-and-regrasp`** on a clear central surface (donor places, opens wide, retreats straight, and clears/homes before receiver enters to re-observe and grasp). Reserve **in-air handoffs** for elongated parts (`>= 5 cm` free span), scenes without clear support, or orthogonal 90° donor-vertical / receiver-horizontal clamps on distinct flat-faced heads (pre-stage the partially open receiver first, keep the donor stationary with camera housing turned away, clamp flat parallel faces rather than curved rims, and verify all poses via `robo.ik()`).
- **Release & homing**: confirm support before opening wider than the object's full diagonal (so pad friction cannot drag it on withdrawal), retreat straight with orientation unchanged, and use `home()` for clearance or final reset (not after every local step).

## 3. Fine Delta layer: `delta_grasp`, `align_feature`, and `nudge`
- **`robo.delta_grasp(target, arm, axis=(0,0,-1), standoff_m=0.0, overdrive_m=0.0, opening=None, contact_table=False, lift_dxyz=None, preview=False)`**: compact close-range pickup or in-air handoff clamp from a staged `2.5–4 cm` hover (optional pre-open -> transverse align -> guarded axial approach -> close -> optional guarded `lift_dxyz`). For low-profile tabletop parts, pass `contact_table=True` with a small `overdrive_m`: only the fingertip floor relaxes to `TABLE_Z - 0.004` in `3 mm` steps (arm links stay clear), tabletop stall counts as expected seating before closing, and `lift_dxyz` automatically uses gentle `3 mm` micro-steps on thin holds (`closed_opening <= 0.045`). For close-range in-air handoffs, pass the receiver's pointing `axis`, partial `opening`, and `standoff_m` to clear the donor fingers.
- **`robo.align_feature(feature_pt, target_pt, arm, axis=(0,0,-1), standoff_m=None, stroke_m=None, preview=False)`**: for tight-clearance (`<= 5 mm`) insertion, hanging, plugging, or pouring where grasp offset or gravity hang shifts the held distal feature (`feature_xyz`) from `tcp_pos`. Stage a `1–2 cm` pre-contact hover, measure both held feature and receptor from that post-hover view (`crop_grid` + two-view `triangulate`, or `ray_at_z` at true `z_feature` to avoid oblique parallax), zero transverse eccentricity, and advance with `stop_on_stall=True`. If an axial stroke stalls well short of full depth (`achieved_stroke_m << stroke_m`), back off `3–5 mm` along `-axis` before re-aligning so rim contact does not strip the hold.
- **`robo.nudge(dxyz, arm, dpitch=, dyaw=, droll=, frame="world"|"tcp", stop_on_stall=True, allow_table_contact=False)`**: guarded compact relative step (`5 mm`/action; `3 mm` on tabletop contact or thin holds). `frame="tcp"` with `dxyz=(d, 0, 0)` pushes along the fingers. `stop_reason="contact_or_stall"` is a kinematic progress test, not a force reading: inspect the wrist view, then release/withdraw if seated or back off `3–5 mm` and adjust sideways if misaligned.
- **Hold tracking**: register `ref = object_ref(name, desc)` and use `observe_object` / `observe_hold` for 3D tracking without table-height assumptions (`unknown` is not empty; `image_consistent` is 2D wrist evidence, not 3D retention; never infer held-object pose from table-height geometry).
