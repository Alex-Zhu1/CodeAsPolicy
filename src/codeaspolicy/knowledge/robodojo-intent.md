# Operating knowledge for RoboDojo: 3-View Bimanual Coarse-Code + Fine-Delta

## 1. Frames, reach, and gripper geometry
- **World & table**: `"left"` arm at `-x`, `"right"` at `+x`, `+y` away, `+z` up (m, deg). Bases at `x = ±0.30, y = -0.45`; table at `z = 0.765` m. `robo.task` is authoritative; read object heights from fresh views.
- **TCP vs envelope**: targets `tcp_pos` (`0.145 m` ahead of `eef_pos` along `+x_tcp`). Fingertips extend `+1.3 cm` past TCP (`tcp_z >= 0.780` m down unless tabletop contact). Closed pads span `±1.2 cm` along `close_dir` (`~2.0 cm` thick); `5–7 cm` behind TCP the **crossbar** spans `±5 cm` at `opening=0.0`; **wrist-camera housing** protrudes on `+z_link6` (`pose.axes()[2]`).
- **Gripper**: signed commands (`CLOSE=1`, `OPEN=-1`, `KEEP=0`); measured `opening` is `1.0` open / `0.0` closed (`width ≈ 0.002 + 0.109 * opening` m, `GRIP_ZERO_M ≈ 2.3 mm`). Thin parts (`< 3.5 mm`) read `opening <= 0.01`—verify visually after lift.
- **Orientations & reach**: `pitch` 0 level / 90 down; `yaw` 0 away / +90 left (`-x`); `roll` about `+x_tcp` (`pitch=90`: `yaw` sets jaw line, `roll=0`). Build poses via `robo.pose_from_axes(xyz, point_dir, close_dir, roll_deg=...)` and arcs via `pose.rotated(axis, deg, pivot=...)`. Straight-down (`pitch=90`) reach stays low (`y <= 0.0` at `z=0.80`, `y <= -0.05` at `z=0.90`, unreachable near `z=1.0`); `pitch=45..60` reaches farther/higher, `pitch=0` farther still.

## 2. 3-View Bimanual Scheduling & Coarse Layer (`execute_motion`, `ik`, `ik_both`)
- **3-View Joint Perception & Bimanual Default**: every snapshot captures all 3 views (`robo.show("all")` on decision turns), and **1 step costs the same moving one arm or both**. Localize left/right subgoals from one snapshot instead of serial single-arm loops.
- **Four Bimanual Topologies**:
  1. **Parallel half-zones**: for left (`x < 0`) and right (`x > 0`) targets, verify with `robo.ik_both(left=hover_L, right=hover_R, jaw_symmetry=True, continuous=True)` -> ONE `execute_motion(id, left=..., right=...)` -> `robo.delta_grasp(arm="both", left=..., right=...)` (`independent_stall` finishes uneven grasps together).
  2. **Pipelined retreat ∥ pre-stage**: when sharing a central target, never spend 20+ steps calling `home(arm)` alone—after opening and straight retreat, overlap return and next staging in ONE `execute_motion(id, left=robo.home_pose("left"), right=next_hover)`.
  3. **Joint rendezvous & co-manipulation**: for compact parts (`< ~6–7 cm` or `< ~4 cm` exposed span), prefer **`place-and-regrasp`** on a clear patch. For in-air handoffs (`>= 5 cm` span, no support, or 90° flat clamps), search `(donor_pose, receiver_standoff, receiver_grasp)` via `robo.ik_both(..., jaw_symmetry=True)` (turning `+z_link6` housings away, maximizing `clearance_m`) and move BOTH in ONE `execute_motion`. For shared pushing/bracing, use `keep_relative=True`, `robo.nudge(arm="both", ...)`, or `robo.align_feature(arm="both", independent_stall=False, ...)`.
  4. **Transport ∥ active stereo view**: overlap held-object transport with moving the free wrist camera to a stereo vantage in one `execute_motion`, then `triangulate` in one snapshot.
- **Coarse/Fine split**: use `execute_motion` to reach `2.5–4 cm` hovers (`max_steps=None` on transit/carry); hand `<= 4 cm` contact directly to `delta_grasp`, `align_feature`, `nudge` without `once()`. On `stop_reason == 'segment_complete'`, resume via `robo.resume_motion(id, max_steps=res['recommended_max_steps'])`. Use `holding='keep'` when rotating thin parts.
- **Code-driven `ik`/`ik_both` search (0 steps)**: compute `R_delta = R_obj_goal @ R_obj_start.T` in NumPy, map `(point_dir, close_dir)` to `(R_delta @ point_dir, R_delta @ close_dir)`, chain `seed=prev.joints` with `jaw_symmetry=True`, filter `all(s.ok)`, pick highest `min(s.min_margin_rad)` (or `clearance_m`), and align pointing axis with rotation axis (`pitch=90` for vertical twist) so rotation runs on `joint6`.
- **Dynamic scenes & third-arm turn-taking (`robo.wait`)**: never loop `set_gripper` to pass time; call `robo.wait(steps=..., until_scene_still=True, min_steps=..., still_steps=12)` to advance steps in one snapshot while holding pose. With a demonstrator/opponent third arm, keep BOTH arms at start poses until it finishes its final action AND retracts to rest (`until_scene_still=True` + confirm in `cam_head`)—moving earlier fails the episode.
- **Step-budget & cleanup reserve**: reserve `>= 25–30` steps for cleanup (`reserve_steps=25` on late `execute_motion`, `nudge`, `delta_grasp`, `align_feature`, `wait`). Before any new subgoal/retry, check remaining budget: if insufficient for retry + cleanup, stabilize and finish via `robo.home("both", open_gripper=True)`.
- **Clearance & bimanual cleanup**: lift held parts (`L_below`) straight up so `object_bottom_z = tcp_z - L_below` clears obstacles by `+0.03` m before lateral travel; retreat straight after low strokes. Act in place when relocation is unnecessary; in packed rows use `pitch=90`. Confirm support, open both (`robo.set_gripper("both", robo.OPEN)`), and finish with `robo.home("both")` (or `robo.home("both", open_gripper=True)`).

## 3. Fine Delta layer: `delta_grasp`, `align_feature`, and `nudge`
- **`robo.delta_grasp(...)`**: close-range pickup/handoff from `2.5–4 cm` hover (`arm="both"` supported). Pass `contact_table=True` + small `overdrive_m` for tabletop parts (3 mm steps, micro-lifts `closed_opening <= 0.045`); pass `axis`, `opening`, `standoff_m` for handoffs.
- **`robo.align_feature(...)`**: tight (`<= 5 mm`) insertion/hanging/plugging (`arm="both"`, `independent_stall=True` independent / `False` coupled). Hover `1–2 cm`, measure feature + receptor, zero transverse error, stroke with `stop_on_stall=True`; back off `3–5 mm` along `-axis` on `rim_contact_suspected`.
- **`robo.nudge(...)`**: guarded relative step (`frame="tcp"` `dxyz=(d,0,0)` pushes along fingers; `arm="both"`, `independent_stall=True`, `stop_on_stall=True` stops on kinematic stall—inspect wrist view before releasing or backing off).
