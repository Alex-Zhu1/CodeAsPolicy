# Operating knowledge for RoboDojo: Coarse-Code + Fine-Delta manipulation

## 1. Robot frames, reach envelope, and gripper geometry
- **World frame & table**: two 6-DoF ARX-X5 arms (`"left"` at negative `x`, `"right"` at positive `x`), `y` away from the robot, `z` up (metres, degrees). Bases sit at `x = ±0.30, y = -0.45`; table surface is `z = 0.765` m. `robo.task` is authoritative; read object heights from fresh observations.
- **TCP vs physical gripper envelope**: motion targets `tcp_pos` (finger-pad centre, `0.145 m` ahead of `eef_pos` along `+x_tcp`). Fingertips extend `+1.3 cm` past `tcp_pos` (keep `tcp_z >= 0.780` m when pointing down unless using tabletop contact). Closed pads are `~2.4 cm` wide (`±1.2 cm` along `close_dir`, `~2.0 cm` thick), while `~5–7 cm` behind `tcp_pos` the **fixed jaw-rail crossbar** spans `±5 cm` along `close_dir` even at `opening=0.0`. The protruding **wrist-camera housing** sits on `+z_link6` (`pose.axes()[2]`).
- **Gripper conventions**: commands are signed (`CLOSE=1`, `OPEN=-1`, `KEEP=0`); measured `opening` is `1.0` open / `0.0` closed (`width ≈ 0.002 + 0.109 * opening` m). Minimum closed gap is `GRIP_ZERO_M ≈ 2.3 mm`, so thin parts (`< 3.5 mm`) read `opening <= 0.01` like empty air—verify thin-part possession visually after a short lift.
- **Orientations & reach**: `pitch` 0 level / 90 straight down; `yaw` 0 away / +90 left (`-x`); `roll` turns about `+x_tcp` (at `pitch=90`, `yaw` sets the jaw-closing line and `roll=0`). Build poses with `robo.pose_from_axes(xyz, point_dir, close_dir, roll_deg=...)` and arcs with `pose.rotated(axis, deg, pivot=...)`. Straight-down (`pitch=90`) reach stays low (`y <= 0.0` at `z=0.80`, `y <= -0.05` at `z=0.90`, unreachable near `z=1.0`); `pitch=45..60` reaches farther and higher, level (`pitch=0`) farther still.

## 2. Coarse layer: `execute_motion`, code-driven `ik` search, and transfer strategy
- **Coarse-Code + Fine-Delta split**: use `execute_motion` + `robo.ik` for task logic, arm role selection, and smooth C2 transport to **`2.5–4 cm` pre-action hover poses** (keep loose default tolerances on `"free"` travel; pass `reserve_steps` on late stages). Hand all close-range (`<= 4 cm`) contact to `delta_grasp`, `align_feature`, and `nudge` called directly without `once()`.
- **Budgeting, resume, and holding**: always verify `robo.ik(...).ok` before dispatching `execute_motion`, and leave `max_steps=None` on normal transit/carry (guarding budget with `reserve_steps`) to avoid multi-segment C2 stop/start step tax. `stop_reason == 'segment_complete'` has known effects (do **not** call `robo.reconcile()`); continue with `robo.resume_motion(id, max_steps=res['recommended_max_steps'])` (cumulative cap) or `robo.resume_motion(id)`. Use `holding='keep'` when rotating stepped/thin parts whose jaw opening may settle into `<= 0.01`.
- **Search candidate poses in Python with `robo.ik()` instead of mental 3D math (0 steps)**: never hand-solve 3D rotations in your head or spend a turn testing one guessed orientation. Whenever reorienting an object (flat-to-upright, side seating, axial twist) or staging a transfer, compute rigid start-to-goal rotation in NumPy (`R_delta = R_obj_goal @ R_obj_start.T`, mapping any candidate grasp `(point_dir, close_dir)` to `(R_delta @ point_dir, R_delta @ close_dir)`) and **loop in Python** over candidate grasp axes, ` ±close_dir` signs, tilts, or transfer positions. Chain `seed=prev.joints` across `(hover -> grasp -> lift -> transfer/seat -> twist_end)` with `robo.ik(arm, pose, seed=..., jaw_symmetry=True, twist_rad=..., check_other_arm=True)`, filter chains with `all(s.ok for s in chain)`, pick the highest `min(s.min_margin_rad for s in chain)` (tie-break by smallest `max_delta_rad`), and execute the winning pose in the same cell.
- **Align pointing axis with rotation axis**: point along the required rotation axis (`pitch=90` for a vertical twist) so rotation runs on `joint6` in place rather than sweeping a 6-DOF arc into singularities.
- **Clearance & in-place actions**: when a held object protrudes `L_below` below `tcp_pos`, lift straight up (`+z`) so `object_bottom_z = tcp_z - L_below` clears obstacles by `+0.03` m before lateral travel. After low contact strokes, retreat straight up (`+z`) or reverse along the contact axis before sweeping sideways. Act in place (tip, flip, slide, press) when relocation is unnecessary; inside packed rows, use `pitch=90` (keeping the `±5 cm` crossbar above neighbour tops) with `close_dir` aligned to the stroke.
- **Cross-arm transfer & sequential clearance**: never send both arms into the central zone simultaneously. For compact uniform objects (`< ~6–7 cm` or `< ~4 cm` exposed beyond donor pads), prefer supported **`place-and-regrasp`** on a clear central patch (donor places, opens wide, retreats straight, and clears/homes before receiver enters to re-observe and grasp). Reserve **in-air handoffs** for elongated parts (`>= 5 cm` free span), scenes without clear support, or orthogonal 90° donor-vertical / receiver-horizontal clamps on flat-faced heads (pre-stage the partially open receiver first, keep donor stationary with camera housing turned away, clamp flat parallel faces rather than curved rims, and select poses via a Python `robo.ik()` search loop).
- **Release & homing**: confirm support before opening wider than the object's diagonal, retreat straight with orientation unchanged, and call `home()` for clearance or final reset.

## 3. Fine Delta layer: `delta_grasp`, `align_feature`, and `nudge`
- **`robo.delta_grasp(...)`**: close-range pickup or in-air handoff clamp from a `2.5–4 cm` hover. For low-profile tabletop parts, pass `contact_table=True` with a small `overdrive_m` (relaxes only fingertips to `TABLE_Z - 0.004` in `3 mm` steps, treats tabletop stall as seating before close, and lifts thin holds `closed_opening <= 0.045` in `3 mm` micro-steps). For in-air handoffs, pass receiver pointing `axis`, partial `opening`, and `standoff_m` to clear donor fingers.
- **`robo.align_feature(...)`**: for tight (`<= 5 mm`) insertion, hanging, plugging, or pouring where grasp offset shifts the held distal feature from `tcp_pos`. Stage a `1–2 cm` hover, measure both held feature and receptor from that post-hover view (`crop_grid` + two-view `triangulate`, or `ray_at_z` at true `z_feature`), zero transverse error, and advance with `stop_on_stall=True`. If `rim_contact_suspected` or `achieved_stroke_m << stroke_m`, back off `3–5 mm` along `-axis` before re-aligning so rim contact does not strip the hold.
- **`robo.nudge(...)`**: guarded compact relative step (`frame="tcp"` `dxyz=(d, 0, 0)` pushes along fingers; `stop_on_stall=True` detects kinematic contact/stall without force sensors—inspect wrist view, then release if seated or back off `3–5 mm` and adjust sideways if misaligned).
