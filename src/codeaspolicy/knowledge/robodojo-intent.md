# Operating knowledge for RoboDojo: 3-View Bimanual Coarse-Code + Fine-Delta

## 1. Frames, reach, and gripper geometry
- **World & table**: two 6-DoF ARX-X5 arms (`"left"` at `-x`, `"right"` at `+x`), `+y` away, `+z` up (metres, deg). Bases at `x = ±0.30, y = -0.45`; table at `z = 0.765` m. `robo.task` is authoritative; read object heights from fresh views.
- **TCP vs envelope**: motion targets `tcp_pos` (pad centre, `0.145 m` ahead of `eef_pos` along `+x_tcp`). Fingertips extend `+1.3 cm` past `tcp_pos` (keep `tcp_z >= 0.780` m pointing down unless using tabletop contact). Closed pads span `±1.2 cm` along `close_dir` (`~2.0 cm` thick); `5–7 cm` behind `tcp_pos` the **fixed crossbar** spans `±5 cm` along `close_dir` even at `opening=0.0`. The protruding **wrist-camera housing** sits on `+z_link6` (`pose.axes()[2]`).
- **Gripper**: signed commands (`CLOSE=1`, `OPEN=-1`, `KEEP=0`); measured `opening` is `1.0` open / `0.0` closed (`width ≈ 0.002 + 0.109 * opening` m). Minimum closed gap is `GRIP_ZERO_M ≈ 2.3 mm`, so thin parts (`< 3.5 mm`) read `opening <= 0.01`—verify visually after a short lift.
- **Orientations & reach**: `pitch` 0 level / 90 down; `yaw` 0 away / +90 left (`-x`); `roll` about `+x_tcp` (at `pitch=90`, `yaw` sets jaw line, `roll=0`). Build poses with `robo.pose_from_axes(xyz, point_dir, close_dir, roll_deg=...)` and arcs with `pose.rotated(axis, deg, pivot=...)`. Straight-down (`pitch=90`) reach stays low (`y <= 0.0` at `z=0.80`, `y <= -0.05` at `z=0.90`, unreachable near `z=1.0`); `pitch=45..60` reaches farther/higher, `pitch=0` farther still.

## 2. 3-View Bimanual Scheduling & Coarse Layer (`execute_motion`, `ik`, `ik_both`)
- **3-View Joint Perception & Bimanual Default**: every snapshot captures `cam_head`, `cam_left_wrist`, and `cam_right_wrist` together (`robo.show("all")` attaches all 3 views on decision turns), and **1 step costs the same moving one arm or both**. Localize left-zone and right-zone subgoals from the same 3-view snapshot instead of serial single-arm loops.
- **Four Bimanual Topologies**:
  1. **Parallel half-zones**: for independent left (`x < 0`) and right (`x > 0`) targets, verify with `robo.ik_both(left=hover_L, right=hover_R, jaw_symmetry=True, continuous=True)` -> ONE `execute_motion(id, left=..., right=...)` -> `robo.delta_grasp(arm="both", left=..., right=...)` (`independent_stall` finishes uneven tabletop grasps together).
  2. **Pipelined retreat ∥ pre-stage**: when sharing a narrow central target/fixture, never spend 20+ steps calling `home(arm)` alone while the other arm waits—after opening and a short straight retreat, overlap return and next staging in ONE `execute_motion(id, left=robo.home_pose("left"), right=next_hover)`.
  3. **Joint rendezvous & co-manipulation**: forbid only *uncoordinated* central crowding. For compact parts (`< ~6–7 cm` or `< ~4 cm` exposed span), prefer supported **`place-and-regrasp`** on a clear patch (place, open, retreat straight, overlap return with receiver staging). For in-air handoffs (elongated `>= 5 cm` span, no clear support, or orthogonal flat-face clamps), search `(donor_pose, receiver_standoff, receiver_grasp)` pairs with `robo.ik_both(..., jaw_symmetry=True)` (turning both `+z_link6` camera housings away and maximizing `clearance_m`), then move BOTH arms in ONE `execute_motion`. For shared rigid-body pushing/bracing, use `keep_relative=True`, `robo.nudge(arm="both", ...)`, or `robo.align_feature(arm="both", independent_stall=False, ...)`.
  4. **Transport ∥ active stereo view**: overlap held-object coarse transport with moving the free wrist camera to a stereo vantage pose in the same `execute_motion`, then `triangulate` in one snapshot.
- **Coarse/Fine split & budgeting**: use `execute_motion` to reach **`2.5–4 cm` pre-action hover poses** (default tolerances on `"free"` travel; `max_steps=None` on normal transit/carry with `reserve_steps` on late stages). Hand close-range (`<= 4 cm`) contact directly to `delta_grasp`, `align_feature`, `nudge` without `once()`. `stop_reason == 'segment_complete'` has known effects (no `reconcile()`); resume via `robo.resume_motion(id, max_steps=res['recommended_max_steps'])` or `robo.resume_motion(id)`. Use `holding='keep'` when rotating thin/stepped parts.
- **Code-driven `ik`/`ik_both` search (0 steps)**: never hand-solve 3D rotations. Compute `R_delta = R_obj_goal @ R_obj_start.T` in NumPy, map `(point_dir, close_dir)` to `(R_delta @ point_dir, R_delta @ close_dir)`, loop over candidate axes/signs/tilts/rendezvous poses chaining `seed=prev.joints` with `jaw_symmetry=True`, filter `all(s.ok for s in chain)`, pick highest `min(s.min_margin_rad for s in chain)` (or dual `clearance_m`), and execute in the same cell. Align pointing axis with rotation axis (`pitch=90` for vertical twist) so rotation runs on `joint6`.
- **Clearance & bimanual cleanup**: lift held parts (`L_below`) straight up so `object_bottom_z = tcp_z - L_below` clears obstacles by `+0.03` m before lateral travel; retreat straight (`+z`/`-axis`) after low strokes. Act in place (tip/flip/slide/press) when relocation is unnecessary; inside packed rows use `pitch=90` (crossbar above neighbours). Confirm support before opening, open both via `robo.set_gripper("both", robo.OPEN)`, and finish with `robo.home("both")`.

## 3. Fine Delta layer: `delta_grasp`, `align_feature`, and `nudge`
- **`robo.delta_grasp(...)`**: close-range pickup or handoff clamp from `2.5–4 cm` hover (supports `arm="both"` with `left`/`right`). For low tabletop parts pass `contact_table=True` + small `overdrive_m` (`3 mm` steps, tabletop stall seats before close, micro-lifts thin holds `closed_opening <= 0.045`). For handoffs pass `axis`, partial `opening`, and `standoff_m`.
- **`robo.align_feature(...)`**: tight (`<= 5 mm`) insertion/hanging/plugging/pouring (`arm="both"` supports `independent_stall=True` for independent fixtures or `False` for coupled bodies). Hover `1–2 cm`, measure held feature + receptor (`crop_grid` + `triangulate` or `ray_at_z`), zero transverse error, stroke with `stop_on_stall=True`. On `rim_contact_suspected`, back off `3–5 mm` along `-axis` before re-aligning.
- **`robo.nudge(...)`**: guarded relative step (`frame="tcp"` `dxyz=(d,0,0)` pushes along fingers; `arm="both"` with `independent_stall=True` finishes uneven contact; `stop_on_stall=True` detects kinematic stall—inspect wrist view before releasing or backing off `3–5 mm`).
